Ahmed Amine Jawhari

Research Economist · PhD Candidate, University of New Mexico

I study how environmental risks and public policy shape cities and well-being. My current work combines environmental & resource economics, urban economics, and applied econometrics to quantify health and housing externalities and to design cost-effective mitigation.

Environmental & Resource Econ Urban Econ Public Econ Applied Econometrics

Research agenda

Measuring how wildfire smoke and other environmental risks impact health and housing markets; valuing mitigation; and mapping the distribution of benefits across neighborhoods.

  • Non-market valuation & contingent valuation
  • Spatial & panel econometrics
  • Policy design & cost-effectiveness

Current projects

Public & Environmental Economics

  • Wildfire Smoke & Housing — price and rent capitalization of smoke exposure; welfare & policy implications.
  • CHW Program at UNM Hospital — evaluating whether community health workers reduce avoidable utilization and costs.

Methods & tools

Quasi-experimental designs (DiD, event studies, IV), spatial models, survey design, and evidence synthesis.

Stata · Python · R · GIS · LaTeX

  • Wildfire Smoke and Urban Housing Markets — draft available.

Updates
