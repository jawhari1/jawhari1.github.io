Peer-Reviewed Articles

Jawhari, Ahmed Amine, Benjamin A. Jones, and Robert P. Berrens. "Linking Forests to Airsheds: Investigating Public Support and Willingness-to-Pay for Reducing Wildfire Smoke Exposure." Land Economics, in press.

A regional contingent valuation study estimating households' willingness-to-pay for wildfire smoke mitigation across 11 Western U.S. states. Results provide benefit estimates relevant for benefit–cost analysis of forest fuel treatment programs and regional air quality policy.
