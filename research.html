Community Health Workers at UNM Hospital

I evaluate whether a Community Health Workers (CHW) program at the University of New Mexico Hospital reduces avoidable utilization and healthcare expenditures. The project links electronic health records, program enrollment data, and billing claims to measure clinical and financial outcomes.

  • Design: Difference-in-differences with matched controls and staggered program entry; event-study analyses to trace dynamic effects.
  • Outcomes: Emergency department visits, inpatient admissions, readmissions, and total costs; heterogeneity by baseline risk and neighborhood disadvantage.
  • Status: Analysis in progress; preliminary results indicate reductions in avoidable utilization and evidence of cost offsets.
