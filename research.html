Community Health Workers at UNM Hospital

I study whether a Community Health Workers (CHW) program at UNM Hospital reduces avoidable utilization and expenditures. The project links EHR encounters, program enrollments, and claims to measure clinical and financial outcomes.

  • Design: difference-in-differences with matched controls and staggered entry; event-study to trace dynamic effects.
  • Outcomes: ED visits, inpatient admissions, readmissions, and total costs; heterogeneous effects by baseline risk & neighborhood disadvantage.
  • Status: analysis in progress; preliminary results suggest reductions in avoidable utilization and cost offsets.
